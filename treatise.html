The treatise

Names do not create what they name, and removing a name does not remove what it named.

One sentence, and a book spent earning it. Institutions mistake their description of reality for reality, then reorganize around the description — and the reorganizing changes the paperwork, not the thing.

Changing the sign on the building does not change what happens inside the building.

Every tradition worth reading inoculates against this at line one. Korzybski said it plainly; the Zen literature spent centuries on it; postmodernism made a career of it. And every one of those traditions then got reified by its own readers into exactly the kind of fixed thing it warned about. The warning does not take. That failure — not the insight, the failure of the insight to stick — gives the book its subject.

“The name is not the thing.”

Repeated for a century. Absorbed by almost nobody.

The operation

Four steps, run on one institution at a time.

  • Take the label an institution applies to itself.
  • Ask what it names — what would observably differ if the label came off.
  • Show the gap.
  • Map the contradictions. Not a verdict. The specific places the institution's own commitments disagree with each other.

The fourth step does the work, and it marks where this departs from criticism. A verdict ends a conversation; the reader either accepts it or does not, and either way the thinking stops. A contradiction map starts one — and a reader can check it against the institution's own documents, without taking the author's word for anything.

The first case study runs the operation on a label that denies belonging to a denomination, and follows the schism trees back to the Second Temple period.

Two rules that keep it from becoming what it studies

Every thesis defeats itself

Read as a category claim, each numbered thesis contradicts itself. That reads as a bug until you try to fix it: the only exit drops the demand that reality sort into categories at all. So the form demonstrates the argument rather than asserting it. Kōans run this mechanic. So does Zhuangzi. So, in its shape, does a disputation nailed to a door — hand the reader a problem, never a conclusion.

The instrument turns on itself, in the shipped text

The final case study runs the operation on this book. A treatise about institutions mistaking their labels for reality, which exempts its own label, refutes itself quietly and completely. So the self-examination ships with the book, or the book does not ship.

A map that excludes itself stopped mapping the territory.

What's coming

The skeleton stands: the argument, the ladder it climbs, the per-chapter engine, the lineage, and the first case study. The numbered theses sit deliberately empty — they need the vocabulary Part I builds before they can say anything, and writing them early would mean writing them twice.

Prose comes last here, on purpose. No date, and no estimate masquerading as one.
