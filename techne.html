Tools, in the open

Techne.

Seven Obsidian plugins, written for a vault that has to carry a lot of reading. Each one got written because something got in the way first, and each one sits on GitHub for anyone who hits the same wall.

Eleven Days One date across eleven calendars at once, Gregorian through Discordian. The moon phase comes with it. Hemi-Sync Helper Logs a Gateway session into the daily note — wave, track, and a focus level that fills itself in. Digital Dogear One-line dogears for whatever you read or watch, with ranges, sequence breaks and a heatmap. External Attachments Keeps attachments on another drive and renders them as though they never moved. Wikilinks survive. Wikipedia to Obsidian Pulls an article in as a clean note: sections picked, tables flattened, references turned into footnotes. E-Reader Transcriber Turns e-reader screenshots into dated, searchable notes through local OCR, overlaps deduplicated. Daily Note Lookback Pulls past daily notes in on demand instead of at open time, so a large vault stops lagging on a phone.

Why these exist

Reading at any volume turns into a filing problem. Most of these came out of that — a way to keep a note, find it again a year later, or stop a vault from stalling on a phone. One of them counts the day in eleven calendars, which solves nothing and earns its place anyway.
