The practice

Consulting.

The work goes up first, in public and at no charge. Whatever this practice offers follows from it, and follows in that order deliberately.

A practice that names a price before it has shown its reasoning asks a stranger to take the reasoning on faith.

This one shows the reasoning first — the videos, the measurements, the treatise, the novel. That asks nothing of a reader, and anyone who wants a conversation afterwards already knows what they would walk into.

So the page carries no tiers, no rates and no booking. When the practice settles on a shape, it appears here, with what it costs stated plainly.

Anyone holding a question that will not wait can write to the address below. Expect a conversation rather than an intake form.

The free work has to earn the conversation.
Not the other way around.
