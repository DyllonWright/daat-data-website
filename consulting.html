Still working this part out.

This page used to list three tiers and two booking buttons. The buttons went nowhere, and the tiers described a practice that had not yet decided what it does.

So both came off, rather than getting wired up.

The work on this site arrives free, and that order holds deliberately. A practice that sells a session before it has shown anything asks a stranger to trust a price list. A practice that publishes the videos, the measurements, the treatise and the novel first asks nothing, and whoever still wants to talk afterwards already knows what they would talk to.

When a shape emerges, it emerges here, with whatever it costs stated plainly. Until then this page promises nothing, because anything it promised now would describe guesswork.

Someone with a question that genuinely will not wait can write to the address below. Expect a conversation, not an intake form.

The free work has to earn the conversation.
Not the other way around.
