Three ways to engage

One intake conversation plus one synthesis report. Fits a bounded question or a first look at how this practice works.

Tier Two

Multi-Week Program

A fixed arc of sessions built on a shared, evolving synthesis report. Fits a sustained transition or a recurring pattern worth real time.

Tier Three

Ongoing

A standing monthly or biweekly cadence with no fixed end date. Fits a client who wants continued comparative and systems support as a regular practice.
